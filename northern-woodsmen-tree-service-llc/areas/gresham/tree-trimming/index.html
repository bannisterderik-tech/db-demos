Questions people actually ask

Is tree trimming something I could do myself?

Some of it. Topping a tree is not pruning — it forces weak regrowth that fails later. Proper reduction cuts back to a lateral, which is why a pruned tree still looks like a tree.

Do you charge to come out and look?

No. For most tree trimming jobs photos are enough to quote it. If it needs eyes on it, the look is free.
