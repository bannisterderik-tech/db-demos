Working in Sherwood

Sherwood housing is 1990s–2010s newer construction, historic Old Town. Permits for structural work go through City of Sherwood. It's about a 35 min drive from Wilsonville.

Portland metro weather sets the schedule: about 42 inches a year, concentrated November through April, then dry stretches through July and August. The heavy tree canopy over older neighborhoods means constant debris load and shaded, slow-drying surfaces.
