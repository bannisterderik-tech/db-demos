Questions people actually ask

Are you insured?

Yes — and ask any tree company for a certificate before they climb. Tree work carries the highest insurance rates in the trades for a reason, and uninsured crews shift that risk to your homeowner's policy.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Most removals can be quoted from photos plus a look at access, since access is what drives the price as much as the tree does.

Are you licensed?

Oregon CCB #259429. Oregon also licenses arborists separately — worth asking about for pruning and health work.

What happens to the wood?

Your call. It can be bucked and stacked for firewood, chipped for mulch, or hauled off entirely.
