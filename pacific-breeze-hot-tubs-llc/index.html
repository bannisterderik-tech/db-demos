Questions people actually ask

Do you handle permits?

Yes — drawings, submittal, and inspections are coordinated as part of the job. That's most of what a general contractor is actually for.

How do you handle change orders?

In writing, priced, and signed before the work happens. Verbal change orders are how projects go sideways.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Oregon CCB #259493. That record shows bond and insurance status too, and you can pull it up in about ten seconds.

How do payments work?

A deposit to schedule, then progress payments tied to completed milestones. Never pay a large percentage up front to anyone.
