Questions people actually ask

Do you use my water?

Standard practice is a hookup to an outside spigot. If water access is a problem, say so up front and it can be worked around.

Will pressure washing damage my siding?

It can, which is why siding gets soft-washed — low pressure plus a cleaning solution. Anyone offering to pressure wash siding at full pressure should be a hard no.

Are you licensed and insured?

Oregon CCB #259581. Worth checking for anyone working around your roof and windows.

How often should this be done?

Gutters twice a year here. Roof moss treatment every two to three years. House washing every one to two years depending on shade and tree cover.
