Questions people actually ask

Do you move furniture?

Standard furniture is part of the job. Pianos, safes, and pool tables get quoted separately because they need specialists.

How long until I can walk on it?

Tile is 24 hours before light traffic, 72 before heavy. Refinished hardwood needs longer — plan on staying off it several days depending on the finish.

Are you licensed?

Oregon CCB #259374. Check it on the CCB site; any legitimate contractor gives you their number without hesitating.

Do you supply material or do I?

Either works. If you're supplying it, order 10–15% extra for cuts and waste — a mid-job reorder from a different lot rarely matches.
