Questions people actually ask

Do you come to me?

Yes — mobile welding is most of the work. Equipment that's broken generally can't be hauled in, which is the whole point.

How do you charge?

Hourly plus travel for repair work; flat quote for fabrication where the scope is known up front.

Are you licensed?

Oregon CCB #259624. Structural work may also require certified welding procedures depending on the permit.

Can you match existing metalwork?

Usually. Send photos — matching an existing profile is mostly a question of stock availability.

Need it fixed?

Stellar Welding works throughout Portland Metro. Call, text, or send photos of the problem and you'll get a straight number back.
