Questions people actually ask

Do you handle the permits?

For septic and anything DEQ-regulated, the permit and site evaluation come first and I'll walk you through what's needed. County permits for driveways and structures are usually the owner's, but you won't be guessing at it.

How do you price a job?

Site visit, then a flat quote. Excavation surprises come from what's under the surface, so if something unexpected turns up you hear about it before the meter runs, not after.

Are you licensed and bonded?

Yes — Oregon CCB #259368. Verify it yourself on the CCB website; that's what it's there for.

How soon can you start?

Depends on weather and ground conditions more than schedule. Wet season limits what can be moved without wrecking a site — sometimes waiting three weeks saves money.
